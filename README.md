### Hi, I'm Rick

I make small, free Windows (occasionally Android/Phone/Web) apps — 
mostly things I wanted and couldn't find.
No ads, no accounts, no telemetry; 
usually one file you can copy anywhere and delete when you're done.

**3D printing**

- **[Vaseinator](https://github.com/RickUsingGitHub/vaseinator)** — designs vases for a printer's
  spiral vase mode: one continuous wall, watertight, no supports. Exports STL and multi-colour 3MF.
- **[Maze Tube Designer](https://github.com/RickUsingGitHub/maze-tube-designer)** — cylinder maze
  puzzle boxes. The maze hides under the cap while you solve it, with a twist lock at the end.
- **[Lenticular Sign Maker](https://github.com/RickUsingGitHub/lenticular-sign-maker)** — signs that
  read (for example, for your dishwasher) **CLEAN** one way and **DIRTY** the other, printed in several colours.

**Learning and music**

- **[Nihongo Michi](https://github.com/RickUsingGitHub/nihongo-michi)** — Japanese from zero to JLPT
  N1: 123 lessons, role-play conversations, all 2,230 JLPT kanji with stroke order, spaced
  repetition. Windows, iPhone and Android.
- **[TABifier](https://github.com/RickUsingGitHub/TABifier)** — sheet music, typed TAB and chord
  charts into Score + TAB for guitar, ukulele and bass (and any other string instrument). Windows and macOS.

**Energy and evidence**

- **[Amber](https://github.com/RickUsingGitHub/Amber)** — usage, costs and bill checking for Amber
  Electric's wholesale pricing, in the browser.
- **[TheTotalCost](https://github.com/RickUsingGitHub/TheTotalCost)** — owner cost and environmental
  cost per kilometre: mid-size ICE versus BEV.
- **[Big Pharma on Trial](https://github.com/RickUsingGitHub/BigPharma)** and
  **[Big Food on Trial](https://github.com/RickUsingGitHub/BigFood)** — independent evidence
  summaries from court records, regulator decisions and peer-reviewed research.

All of it is free. If something here has saved you time or money, you can
